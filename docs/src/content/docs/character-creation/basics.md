## Overview

- 1. Roll for your character’s first and last Name as well as Age (2d20+10).
- 2. Roll for your characters’ Attributes and Hit Protection. (pg. 27)
- 3. Roll for your character’s Traits. (pg. 28)
- 4. Roll for your character’s Background and related items. (pg. 30)
- 5. Roll for your character’s Weapon & Armor. (pg. 33)
- 6. Roll for an Additional Gear to complete your character’s starting equipment. (pg. 34)
- 7. Purchase any additional items your character can afford from the Marketplace. (pg. 37)

Hit Protection

• Roll 1d6 to determine your PC’s starting Hit Protection (HP).
